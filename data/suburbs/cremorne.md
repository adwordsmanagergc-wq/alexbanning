Cremorne is a compact harbour suburb between Neutral Bay and Mosman. Its best known feature is Cremorne Point, a narrow headland with a foreshore reserve and walking track that looks across the water to the city, the Opera House and the harbour bridge. A ferry wharf at the point connects residents directly to Circular Quay.

Military Road runs along the top of the suburb and holds its shops, restaurants and the Hayden Orpheum Picture Palace, the restored art deco cinema that has become a landmark in its own right. Buses along the road link Cremorne with North Sydney and the city, and the harbour is a short walk downhill from most streets.

The housing stock leans towards apartments. Art deco and interwar blocks are a defining feature, many with generous rooms, high ceilings and original detail. There are also modern apartment buildings, a number of federation houses and semi detached homes, and harbourfront residences on the point and the slopes around it. On the water side, even modest apartments can have a strong outlook.

Buyers are a mix of professionals, couples, downsizers from larger homes nearby and families who want the harbour without the size of a full house. Many already live in Cremorne or the neighbouring suburbs and know which buildings and streets they prefer. For apartments, the building matters as much as the unit itself.

**Selling well here depends on detail.** Photography should capture light, outlook and the character of older buildings. Strata information, including reports, levies and any planned works, should be ready from day one. Houses and harbourfront homes benefit from a campaign that shows the setting, from the foreshore walk to the village. A clear, honest presentation of the view and aspect builds trust with buyers who know the area well.

## FAQ

### What do buyers look for in Cremorne apartments?
Light, aspect, layout and the quality of the building. Parking and storage matter, as does walking distance to the ferry, buses and Military Road. Buyers will review strata records closely, so have minutes, levies and any capital works plans ready. Well maintained common areas and a harmonious strata committee help a sale more than many sellers expect.

### Is auction or private treaty better in Cremorne?
Auction is common for well presented apartments and houses with broad appeal, because it concentrates competition. Private treaty can suit homes with specific appeal or where buyers need more time, such as properties with complex strata or planning matters. The best choice depends on the property and the interest shown early in the campaign.

### Should I renovate my art deco apartment before selling?
Often not. Many buyers value original detail and prefer to renovate to their own taste. Focus on repairs, fresh paint, clean floors and good lighting. If the kitchen or bathroom is in poor condition, a modest refresh may help, but a full renovation rarely returns its cost. Ask your agent which improvements buyers in your building are likely to value.

### When is the best time to sell in Cremorne?
Spring and early autumn are traditionally active, and harbour light often shows well in those seasons. Apartments and houses with good outlooks can sell well throughout the year. Timing around your own plans, the level of competing stock and any strata works in your building is often more important than the season.
