Greenwich is a peninsula suburb where the Lane Cove River meets the harbour. It feels further from the city than it is. The streets narrow as they run south towards Greenwich Point, where a ferry wharf offers a direct and unhurried route into Sydney, and the shoreline is lined with bays, reserves and moored boats.

The suburb has a strong heritage character. Victorian and federation cottages and larger period homes line many of the older streets, often within conservation areas. Sandstone walls, established gardens and water glimpses between the houses give the lower streets a village feel. Greenwich Baths, a netted harbour pool, is a much loved local spot through the warmer months.

Further north, towards the Pacific Highway and St Leonards, the character shifts. There are interwar homes, post-war houses and apartment buildings, with easier access to the rail line, the hospitals and the commercial centre of St Leonards. Greenwich Road and River Road carry most of the local traffic, leaving the side streets quiet.

The buyer pool reflects this range. Families and professionals seeking a heritage house near the water are a core group, alongside downsizers who want a smaller home or apartment without leaving the area. Buyers who work in the city or North Sydney are drawn by the combination of ferry, bus and rail access. Many are already familiar with Greenwich and the neighbouring suburbs.

For sellers, heritage and water are the two threads to draw out. **Accurate information on conservation controls** and any past approvals helps buyers commit with confidence. Photography should show the house in its setting, including the garden, the street and any outlook. For waterfront homes, have details on structures and approvals ready. A well prepared, honest campaign suits a market where buyers know exactly what they are looking for.

## FAQ

### Is auction or private treaty better in Greenwich?
Auction often suits heritage houses and family homes with broad appeal, where competition can be focused on one day. Private treaty can suit waterfront properties, homes needing major work or apartments with a narrower buyer pool. Your agent should recommend a method after assessing early interest, rather than defaulting to one approach.

### How does heritage affect a sale in Greenwich?
Heritage controls shape what buyers can change, which matters to anyone planning an extension or rebuild. Many Greenwich buyers seek heritage homes and accept these limits, but they want clarity. Provide any heritage listing details, reports and approvals for past works early in the campaign so buyers can assess the property with confidence.

### What do buyers look for in Greenwich?
Character, a quiet street, some sense of the water and good access to the city. Families look for bedrooms, outdoor space and parking, while downsizers look for single level living, easy maintenance and walkability. Across all groups, a well maintained home with clear information tends to attract stronger and faster interest.

### Should I sell off market in Greenwich?
An off market sale can suit sellers who value privacy or who already have a likely buyer. The trade off is reduced exposure, which can mean a lower price if the best buyer never sees the home. Many sellers use a brief private phase to test interest, then move to a public campaign if needed.
