FAQs

Longueville appraisal questions.

How much is my Longueville home worth?

The honest answer is that it depends on the street, aspect, condition and the buyer pool active for that property type. Alex provides a precise, evidence-based figure after a short on-site visit, drawing on recent comparable Longueville sales — including off-market transactions that don’t appear on the major portals.

Is the appraisal really free?

Yes. Alex’s market appraisals are complimentary and obligation-free, whether you are twelve months from selling or simply curious about your home’s ceiling.

What’s the difference between a market appraisal and a bank valuation?

A market appraisal is the price an experienced local agent expects your home to achieve in the current market. A bank valuation is a more conservative figure used by lenders for mortgage security. The two are rarely the same number, and serve different purposes.

How long does an appraisal take?

The on-site visit usually takes 20–30 minutes. You’ll typically receive a written appraisal within two business days.

Can my Longueville home be sold off-market?

Yes — and in many cases it should be. A meaningful share of Longueville’s strongest results are achieved through pre-market and off-market campaigns to a curated buyer database. Alex will advise honestly on whether public, hybrid or off-market is most likely to deliver your strongest result.

How should I prepare my Longueville home for sale?

Alex provides a tailored pre-market plan covering styling, minor works, photography and timing — calibrated to your home’s likely audience and price point. There is rarely a one-size-fits-all answer.
