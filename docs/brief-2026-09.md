## 1. The brief

Rebuild **alex-banning.com** as a quiet-luxury personal brand site for **Alex Banning, Partner Agent & Director, Raine & Horne Lower North Shore** (Sydney).

**The strategic job:** Alex is the dominant apartment agent in Lane Cove (57 of his last 60 sales were apartments). The site must keep that credibility but **reposition him upward**, so owners of $3M to $10M+ houses in Chatswood West, Longueville, Northbridge, Castlecrag, Mosman, Cremorne and Hunters Hill see him as the natural choice for a prestige campaign.

The positioning line to build everything around:

**"The Lower North Shore's most recommended agent. Now representing the area's finest homes."**

How we earn that honestly:
1. Lead with **prestige evidence** (his $4,025,000 Chatswood estate, $2.33M Cremorne record, current 4-bed pool home off-market in Chatswood), not with the $850k two-beds.
2. Reframe the apartment volume as **proof of process and buyer depth**: "60 sales and $60M+ in the last 12 months means the largest active buyer database in Lane Cove. That is what a prestige home needs."
3. Sell **discretion**: off-market and private campaigns (he already runs them, and reviews praise them).
4. Let **volume of reviews** (378 on RateMyAgent, 191 at 5.0 on realestate.com.au) do the talking, not adjectives.

**Tone:** understated, confident, editorial. Think Sotheby's International Realty, The Agency, Ray White Prestige, Aesop, Four Seasons. No exclamation marks, no "dream home", no stock-agent clichés. Short sentences. **Never use em dashes in any copy.**

## 5. Design system

- **Palette:** Ink navy `#0F1B2D` (keep, it is the current brand), warm ivory `#F6F2EA`, stone `#D9D2C5`, deep charcoal `#1A1A1A`, muted brass accent `#B08D57` (lines, small caps labels, hover states only). Raine & Horne yellow appears **only** inside the official R&H logo lockup.
- **Type:** Display serif: Cormorant Garamond or Canela-style (current site uses Cormorant, keep continuity), large sizes, tight tracking. Body: Inter or Söhne-style sans at 17 to 18px, generous 1.7 line height. Small caps tracked labels (e.g. `SIGNATURE SALE · CHATSWOOD`).
- **Layout:** huge whitespace, asymmetric editorial grids, full-bleed photography, thin brass rules, numbers set large in serif ("$4,025,000").
- **Motion:** slow and restrained. Fade/rise on scroll (400 to 600ms, ease-out), gentle Ken Burns on hero stills, horizontal drag gallery for sales. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Components:** sticky minimal header (monogram "AB" + name, turns solid on scroll), floating "Call Alex" pill on mobile, testimonial carousel with counter, results counter band, suburb cards, appraisal multi-step form.
- **Performance:** Lighthouse 95+ on mobile, LCP under 2.0s, all images responsive `srcset`, lazy below the fold, fonts self-hosted with `font-display: swap`.
